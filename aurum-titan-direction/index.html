Aurum Titan Direction: gold direction indicator for MT5

A MetaTrader 5 indicator for manual XAUUSD traders: a direction score from -100 to +100, non-repainting arrows on closed bars, and a suggested stop loss, three targets and lot size.

What Aurum Titan Direction does

Aurum Titan Direction is a MetaTrader 5 indicator for manual traders. It uses the same direction score and components as the Aurum Titan Prop Expert Advisor, and it never places trades itself. No performance figures are published.

Direction score from -100 to +100

The score is calculated on the last closed bar from several weighted components: the chart-timeframe EMA trend, the higher-timeframe EMA trend, price against the value zone, RSI against 50, ADX and the directional indicators, and swing structure. The panel shows the headline (BUY, SELL or NEUTRAL) with a breakdown of each component.

Non-repainting arrows on closed bars

BUY and SELL arrows appear only on closed bars, when the score crosses the signal level (default 60) and the higher timeframe agrees. They do not repaint. The score is also written to an indicator buffer, so it shows in the Data Window.

Suggested stop loss, three targets and lot size

In the current direction the indicator draws an entry at the live price, a stop loss beyond the recent swing extreme plus an ATR buffer, targets at 1R, 2R and 3R, and a lot size for your chosen risk percentage, capped at a maximum lot. If you trade from it, always place the suggested stop loss yourself.

Alerts

Alerts fire on a new arrow and on a direction flip, as pop-ups, push notifications or e-mail.

Requirements and setup

  1. MetaTrader 5 desktop terminal for Windows; works on XAUUSD and other symbols your broker offers.
  2. Copy the delivered .ex5 to MQL5\Indicators, refresh the Navigator and drag it onto a chart. If the Aurum Titan Prop panel is on the same chart, move one panel so they do not overlap.
