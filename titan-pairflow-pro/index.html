Titan PairFlow Pro: z-score pair trading EA for MT5

Multi-asset statistical arbitrage for MetaTrader 5: the EA computes a hedge ratio and a z-score spread index for each pair, then opens and manages both legs in Auto Pilot or Manual mode. Free demo trial available.

What Titan PairFlow Pro does

Titan PairFlow Pro is an Expert Advisor for statistical pair trading on MetaTrader 5. Instead of guessing market direction, it measures how two related instruments move together and trades the spread between them. It works with forex, metals, indices, crypto, stocks and CFDs. No performance figures are published. You can try the engine first with the free demo trial.

Hedge ratio, correlation and z-score

  • For each pair A/B, the EA regresses ln(A) on ln(B) over a lookback of closed bars (OLS). That gives the hedge ratio (beta) and the correlation.
  • The spread is ln(A) minus beta times ln(B). The z-score shows how far the spread is from its mean, in standard deviations.
  • Entries: long the spread (buy A, hedge with B) when z falls to minus the entry threshold; short the spread when z rises to plus the threshold. Signals use closed-bar values and are checked once per new bar.
  • Exits: when the spread reverts (|z| at or below the exit level), on a z stop, on a time stop, on basket money take-profit or stop-loss, or when the loss guard triggers.

Beta-neutral, risk-based sizing

Leg A is sized from your risk money and the distance between the entry and stop z-levels. Leg B is sized from the hedge ratio. Lots use each symbol's own tick size, tick value and lot step, so pairs from different asset classes can be combined. If leg B fails to open, leg A is closed immediately, so no leg is left unhedged.

Up to 20 pairs, Auto Pilot or Manual

  • Auto Pilot (default) opens and manages both legs automatically; Manual mode lets you open and close from the dashboard.
  • Each pair row shows correlation, beta, live z, status and P/L, with buttons L (long spread), S (short spread), X (close) and Z (show the spread index).
  • Quick symbol search: type, press FIND, click matches to fill A and B, then ADD PAIR. Broker suffixes and prefixes (for example XAUUSD.x or GOLD) are resolved automatically.
  • Each pair has its own magic number. On netting accounts, a pair is skipped if one of its symbols already has a position.

Filters and loss guard

Filters: minimum correlation, maximum beta, maximum spread per leg, session hours, high-impact news (MT5 calendar, live only), maximum open pairs and maximum margin as a share of free margin. A daily and total loss guard with saved locks suits prop-firm style accounts.

Important: orders carry no broker-side stop loss. The EA manages all exits itself, so keep MetaTrader 5 running (a VPS helps). Pair trading does not remove market risk: correlations break down and spreads can trend instead of reverting.

Requirements and setup

  1. MetaTrader 5 desktop terminal (build 4000 or later) on Windows.
  2. Two symbols with overlapping trading hours and enough history (by default 200 bars of M15).
  3. Copy the delivered EA .ex5 to MQL5\Experts and the spread-index indicator TitanPairFlowZ.ex5 to MQL5\Indicators, then refresh the Navigator and attach the EA to a chart.
  4. Run it in the Strategy Tester and on a demo account first. Results depend on your broker's prices, costs and execution.
