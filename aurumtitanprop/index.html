Aurum Titan Prop: prop-firm XAUUSD Expert Advisor for MT5

Rule-based MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisor for gold (XAUUSD), built around daily and total loss rules for prop-firm style accounts. Closed-bar trend-pullback entries, a broker-side stop loss and take profit on every order, and an on-chart firm and phase selector.

What Aurum Titan Prop does

Aurum Titan Prop is a fully automated Expert Advisor for XAUUSD (gold) on MetaTrader 5. Its default engine, the Trade Planner, trades trend-pullback setups on closed bars. Around it is a risk guard built for the daily and total loss rules used by prop-firm style accounts. No performance figures are published for this EA. Test it in the Strategy Tester and on a demo account first.

Trade Planner: closed-bar trend and pullback entries

  • Trend: up when the fast EMA (21) is above the slow EMA (50) and price closes above the slow EMA; down is the mirror image. In a range the EA does not trade.
  • Setup: price pulls back into a value zone around the fast EMA (sized by ATR), then a confirmed bar closes back in the trend direction. Each pullback gives at most one signal.
  • Entry and exits: the order goes in at the start of the next bar. The stop loss sits beyond the pullback extreme plus an ATR buffer. Targets are at 1R, 2R and 3R, the position scales out in parts, and the stop moves to break-even after the first target.
  • Size: a percentage of the balance (default 0.5%), never above MaxLot.
  • Broker-side stops: every order is sent with a stop loss and take profit. If the broker leaves one off, the EA tries to set it, and closes the position if it cannot.
  • Account types: works on hedging accounts (three positions, each with its own take profit) and netting accounts (one position, with the first two targets taken as partial closes).

Prop-firm risk guard: daily and total loss locks

  • Daily loss lock: when the loss from the day-start base reaches the limit, the EA closes its positions, alerts you and stops trading until the next daily reset (set in server time).
  • Total loss lock: a total-loss breach closes positions and locks trading permanently until you reset it on purpose.
  • Room check: a new trade is skipped if its stop-loss risk would not fit in the room left before a limit.
  • Discipline limits: maximum trades per day (default 3), a stop after a losing streak (default 3), an optional daily-profit consistency cap, weekend flat on Friday, and an optional session filter.
  • Saved state: guard values are saved, so a restart or re-attach does not reset the day.

Firm and phase presets on the chart

Presets set the daily and total limits (with a safety margin below the firm's numbers), the daily basis, the reset time, phase targets and minimum days. The panel's FIRM and Phase buttons switch between FTMO, FundedNext, The5ers, FundingPips, AquaFunded, Alpha Capital, E8 Markets, Blue Guardian, FXIFY, Maven, Generic 5/10 and Custom, and between Phase 1, Phase 2 and Funded. Switching never closes positions, and existing breach locks stay. An optional challenge-target mode closes the EA's positions and locks trading once the phase target is reached.

The presets are based on publicly available rules, which change often and vary by program. Always check your own firm's current rules. Aurum Titan is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any prop firm.

News, spread and session filters

The news filter uses the MetaTrader 5 economic calendar (USD, high impact by default). It blocks new trades from a set number of minutes before an event until after it, and can close the EA's positions when a news window starts. A spread cap (default 50 points) blocks entries when spreads widen. The calendar is not available in the Strategy Tester, so the news filter is skipped there.

Optional grid engine, off by default

A grid engine is included but locked behind AllowGridModes = false, because most prop firms ban grid and martingale methods. It only runs on hedging accounts when you turn it on yourself.

Panel and alerts

The on-chart panel shows the trend, the planner trade (entry, SL, targets, lot, risk, live R), the active preset, guard status (OK, NEAR LIMIT, LOCKED-DAILY, LOCKED-TOTAL, CONSISTENCY CAP or WEEKEND FLAT), daily and total loss used, trades today, loss streak and the next news event. Buttons: Close Buys, Close Sells, Close All and Pause/Resume. Alerts can go to pop-ups, push notifications or e-mail.

Requirements and setup

  1. MetaTrader 5 desktop terminal for Windows. No DLL imports are needed.
  2. Copy the delivered .ex5 file to MQL5\Experts (File, Open Data Folder), refresh the Navigator, and attach it to an XAUUSD M15 chart (your broker may use a suffix such as XAUUSD.r).
  3. Choose your firm preset and phase, set the server hour of your firm's daily reset, and set the initial account size (or leave 0 to use the balance at first attach).
  4. Test in the Strategy Tester ("Every tick based on real ticks") and on a demo or free-trial account before any paid evaluation or live account.
